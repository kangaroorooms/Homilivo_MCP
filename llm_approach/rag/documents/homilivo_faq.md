What is Homilivo? Homilivo is an online platform for finding rental flats, rooms, single rooms, PG accommodations and independent houses in India. We connect tenants with owners and agents.

How does Homilivo work, and how do I use it step by step? Search rooms by city, budget and room type, or ask the chat assistant. Unlock a property to see more details, choose GOLD or PLATINUM for the city to see the owner's contact information, then contact the owner directly.

Which cities do you cover, and in which cities is Homilivo available? Homilivo lists rental properties in many cities across India, including Udaipur, Bangalore, Jaipur, Kota and Mumbai. Check the city filter on the website for the full list. Plans are priced per city.

Do I need an account? You can browse listings freely. You need an account to unlock a property and see more details.

Is Homilivo free to use? You can start on the FREE plan at ₹0. It lets you unlock up to 3 properties per city with approximate location. Paid plans unlock more.

What does unlocking a property mean? Unlocking a property uses one of the unlocks allowed by your plan in that city, and shows the details your plan includes.

What subscription plans does Homilivo have, which plans do you offer, and what is the difference between the plans? FREE: 3 unlocks per city, approximate location. GOLD: 25 unlocks per city, exact location and owner contact information. PLATINUM: unlimited unlocks, exact location, owner contact information and priority support. Plans and unlocks are per city, so you need a separate plan for each city.

How much do the plans cost, and how much do GOLD and PLATINUM cost? Prices differ by city and the exact price is shown on the pricing page before you pay.

How do I contact an owner? Upgrade to GOLD or PLATINUM for the city, unlock the property, and the owner's contact information is shown. You can also ask our chat assistant, which will show the plans for your city.

How do I pay, and what payment methods do you accept? Payments are processed securely by Razorpay.

My money was deducted but my plan is not active. What should I do? Wait a few minutes, then email support@homilivo.com with your payment or order ID. Failed or duplicate payments are refunded in 5-7 business days.

What is the refund policy, and can I get a refund if I change my mind? Failed or duplicate payments, or a payment of the wrong amount, are refunded in full to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after approval. Once a plan is active and you have used it, the fee is generally non-refundable, even if you change your mind. To request a refund, email support@homilivo.com with your name, registered email or phone number, the payment or order ID, the date and amount, and a screenshot or bank message if you have one.

Is it safe to pay the owner in advance, and how do I avoid rental scams? Always see the property in person, or send someone you trust, before paying anything. Never pay a token, advance or deposit to someone you have not met or for a property you have not seen. Homilivo does not collect rent or deposits: rent and deposits are paid directly to the owner. Homilivo never asks you for rent or deposits and never asks for your OTP, PIN or card details.

Do you collect rent or deposits? Homilivo does not collect rent or deposits and is not part of rental agreements. These are settled directly between you and the owner.

A listing looks wrong or fake. What should I do? Report it at support@homilivo.com with the listing link, and we will look into it.

What can the chat assistant do? It can search rooms for you by city, budget, room type and amenities, sort results, and answer questions about Homilivo and the plans.

Is the assistant always right? The assistant can make mistakes, so please confirm important details on the listing itself or with support. Do not share passwords or card details in the chat.

How do I delete my account? Use the Delete Account page on the website, or email support@homilivo.com from your registered email.

How do you use my data? We use your details to run your account, show you relevant listings, process payments, answer support requests and keep the platform safe. We share details with an owner only when your plan lets you connect with them, and with service providers who work for us, such as Razorpay for payments. See our Privacy Policy for the full details and your rights.

How can I contact support? Email support@homilivo.com.

How do I list my property on Homilivo, and how do owners and agents get started? Owners list a property by signing up (create an account, or use Become an agent in the website footer) and adding the property with clear photos, rent, location and amenities. Tenants on paid plans then see the owner contact information and reach the owner directly. Owners update or remove the listing when the property is no longer available.

Can I search for rooms for students, working professionals or families, or for boys or girls only? Use the Ideal for filter (Students, Working Professionals, Family) and the Gender preference filter (Anyone, Boys only, Girls only) on the search page.

Can I search by budget? Set a minimum and maximum rent on the search page, or choose one of the ready-made budget presets.
