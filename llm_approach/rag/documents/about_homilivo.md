About Homilivo - Who we are: Homilivo is an online platform that helps people find rental homes across India: flats, rooms, single rooms, PG accommodations and houses. We connect tenants directly with verified owners, so finding a place to live is simpler, clearer and safer.

About Homilivo - Why we built it: Finding a rental is often slow and stressful. Listings are outdated, brokers add cost and confusion, and it is hard to know who to trust. Homilivo was built to remove that friction: real listings, direct access to owners, and clear, honest information before you decide.

About Homilivo - What you can do on Homilivo: Search and compare. Browse rental properties by city, budget, room type and amenities. Unlock property details. Every city plan lets you unlock properties; paid plans give you exact location and the owner's contact information. Ask our assistant. Our AI assistant can search rooms for you, narrow results by budget or area, and answer questions about how Homilivo works. List your property. Owners and agents can list properties and reach tenants looking for a home.

About Homilivo - Our plans: Tenants can start free and upgrade when they are ready to connect. Plans are priced per city. FREE: What you get ₹0. Unlock up to 3 properties per city. Approximate location only. GOLD: What you get Unlock up to 25 properties per city. Exact location and owner contact information. PLATINUM: What you get Unlimited property unlocks. Exact location, owner contact information and priority support. Prices differ by city and are shown on the pricing page before you pay.

About Homilivo - Our promise: Verified owners. We work to make sure the people you contact are real owners. Transparent pricing. You see what a plan costs and includes before you pay. Support that answers. Write to us any time at support@homilivo.com.

About Homilivo - An important note: Homilivo is a platform that connects tenants and owners. We are not a party to any rental agreement, and we do not collect or handle rent. Agreements and payments for a tenancy are made directly between the tenant and the owner. Please visit a property and read your agreement carefully before you commit. For help, email support@homilivo.com.
