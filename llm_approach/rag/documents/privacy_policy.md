Homilivo privacy policy - Who we are: This Privacy Policy explains how Homilivo ("Homilivo", "we", "us") collects, uses, shares and protects your personal information when you use homilivo.com and our related services (the "Platform"). By using the Platform you agree to this policy.

Homilivo privacy policy - Information we collect: Account details: your name, email address, phone number and login details (stored in protected form) when you register or sign in. Search and usage data: the cities, budgets, room types and filters you search for; properties you view or unlock; pages visited. Chat data: the messages you send to our AI assistant, and the rooms it shows you, so it can answer in context during your conversation.

Homilivo privacy policy - Information we collect: Listing data (owners and agents): the property details, photos, rent, location and contact details you submit when listing a property. Payment information: the plan, amount, order ID, payment status and time. Payments are processed by Razorpay. Device and technical data: your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system and basic logs, collected automatically.

Homilivo privacy policy - Information we collect: Communications: the emails or messages you send to our support team.

Homilivo privacy policy - How we use your information: To create and manage your account and provide the Platform. To show you relevant listings and to let you unlock properties and connect with owners as your plan allows. To process payments and issue refunds where applicable. To answer your questions through our support team and AI assistant. To keep the Platform safe, prevent fraud and misuse, and enforce our Terms & Conditions. To improve our services, fix errors and understand how the Platform is used.

Homilivo privacy policy - How we use your information: To send service messages (account, payment and security notices). We send marketing messages only where the law allows and you can opt out at any time.

Homilivo privacy policy - Our AI assistant: The Platform includes an AI chat assistant. The assistant keeps the context of your conversation for a short time during your session (about one hour of inactivity) and then it is discarded. Please do not share sensitive personal information such as passwords, card numbers or government ID numbers in the chat. The assistant can make mistakes, so please check important details on the listing itself.

Homilivo privacy policy - Who we share information with: We share it only in these cases: Property owners. When your plan lets you connect with an owner, relevant details such as your name and contact information may be shared with that owner. Service providers who work for us under contract: payment processing (Razorpay), cloud hosting, AI processing, email/SMS delivery and analytics. Legal and safety reasons. Where required by law, court order or government authority, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users and Homilivo.

Homilivo privacy policy - Who we share information with: Business changes. If Homilivo is merged with or acquired by another business, your information may transfer to it, subject to this policy. We do not sell your personal information.

Homilivo privacy policy - Cookies and similar technologies: We use cookies and similar tools to keep you signed in, remember preferences, measure usage and keep the Platform secure. You can control cookies in your browser settings, but parts of the Platform may not work without them.

Homilivo privacy policy - How long we keep information: We keep your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the Platform, meet legal, tax and accounting duties, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

Homilivo privacy policy - Your rights and choices: Access and correct the information in your account. Ask us to delete your account and personal information. You can do this from the Delete Account page on the Platform or by emailing support@homilivo.com. We may keep some records where the law requires. Withdraw your consent or opt out of marketing messages at any time. Ask questions or make a complaint about how we handle your data (see Contact and grievance officer).

Homilivo privacy policy - Security: We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and secure payment processing, to protect your information. No online service can be completely secure, so please keep your login details private and tell us right away if you suspect unauthorised access. If a personal data breach affects you, we will inform you and the authorities as required by law.

Homilivo privacy policy - Children: The Platform is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Homilivo privacy policy - Contact and grievance officer: Questions, requests or complaints about this policy can be sent to: Email: support@homilivo.com.

Homilivo privacy policy - Changes to this policy: We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make an important change, we will tell you on the Platform or by email.

Homilivo privacy policy - Your rights and choices: Under Indian data protection law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can also nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so. If you are not satisfied with how we handle a grievance, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
