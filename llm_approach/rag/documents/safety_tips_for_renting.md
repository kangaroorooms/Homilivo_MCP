Renting safety tips - Before you contact or pay anyone: Check that the photos, rent and location look realistic and consistent. Be careful with prices that seem too good to be true. Ask the owner questions on a call and note whether the answers match the listing. Use the details Homilivo shows you, and keep conversations about the property in writing where possible.

Renting safety tips - Visit the property: Always see the property in person, or send someone you trust, before paying anything. Meet the owner or the person with authority to rent it out and ask to see proof of ownership or authority. Go during the day when possible, and tell a friend or family member where you are going.

Renting safety tips - Paying money: Never pay a token, advance or deposit to someone you have not met or for a property you have not seen. Rent and deposits are paid directly to the owner. Homilivo never asks you for rent or deposits and never asks for your OTP, PIN or card details. Pay Homilivo only through the official website. Check you are on homilivo.com before you pay for a plan. Ask for a receipt for every payment you make to an owner.

Renting safety tips - The agreement: Read the rental agreement fully before signing. Check rent, deposit, notice period, maintenance and rules. Make sure the name on the agreement matches the owner. Keep copies of everything.

Renting safety tips - Report problems: If a listing looks fake, an owner asks for money before a visit, or someone pretends to be from Homilivo, stop and write to us at support@homilivo.com with the listing link and details. If you have lost money, also report it to your bank and to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal, or call the cyber crime helpline 1930.

Renting safety tips - Tips for owners: Share only the details needed to rent the property and verify tenants before handing over keys. Do not accept payments through links or QR codes sent by strangers, and never share your OTP or banking PIN. Use a written agreement and keep records of rent and deposit.
